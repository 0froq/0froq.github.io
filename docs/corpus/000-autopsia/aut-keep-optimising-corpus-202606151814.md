## The 6 Layers

Now we have 6 layers: _**Autopsia**_, _**Ingesta**_, _**Neoplasma**_,
_**Putredo**_, _**Delirium**_, _**Vigil**_.
Each layer is designed for:

- aut: seft-reflection, metacognition;
- ing: captures of the outter information, without judgement;
- neo: inner thoughts;
- put: daily logs, project logs, reflections;
- del: collections and creations that help me survive;
- vig: nightly journals, with less limitations on topic and format.

And we have some issues on:

- No life-cycle. Records stucked at ing and neo layer,
  and has less relation with put.
  - Most of the ings are paper notes and book excerpts.
  - Most of the neos are thoughts about excerpts and not growing.
  - Most of the puts are only project or daily records.
  - A large part of the auts are AI-generated things.
