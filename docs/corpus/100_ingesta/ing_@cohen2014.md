> The Arctic region has warmed ==more than twice as fast as the global average==
> — a phenomenon known as Arctic amplification.
> The rapid Arctic warming has contributed to dramatic melting
> of Arctic sea ice and spring snow cover,
> at a pace greater than that simulated by climate models.
