## Introduction

Current research mainly focuses on comparing the trends of hiatus with pre-hiatus,
but **post-hiatus** trends are less studied.

## Results

- Post-hiatus trends are different from pre-hiatus trends. Should be higher.
- Trends are spatially heterogeneous. Trends changes are heterogeneous as well.
  Try to explain the spatial heterogeneity.
- 2015 - 2018 is a platform, but not the continuation of the hiatus.
  It should be due to the interannual variability. (NEED TO BE TESTED,
